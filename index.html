Route 01

Middle East to Europe

Syria → Turkey → Greece / Italy → Germany

Civil war and persecution drive flight from Syria [6]. Turkey hosts 2.3M and Germany 2.7M people across all origins (UNHCR, mid-2026 [1]); Syrians are among those hosted. Sea crossings risk shipwreck: 1,842 dead or missing were recorded in the Central Mediterranean in 2024, across all nationalities (IOM–UNHCR, 2025 [3]). Greece and Italy are entry points; some continue to Germany.
